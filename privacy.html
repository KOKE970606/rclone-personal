Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Scope

This policy applies to Personal Drive Backup, a private, single-user Google Drive integration operated through rclone on the owner's computer.

Google user data accessed

Depending on the permissions granted by the owner, rclone may access Google Drive files, folders, and associated metadata needed to list, copy, upload, download, compare, synchronize, or delete files at the owner's direction.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to perform backup, restore, transfer, and synchronization operations explicitly initiated or scheduled by the owner. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any unrelated purpose.

Storage and security

  • File processing takes place locally on the owner's computer and directly between that computer and Google Drive.
  • OAuth credentials are stored locally in the owner's rclone configuration.
  • This public website does not receive or store Google Drive files, OAuth credentials, or personal information.

Sharing and disclosure

Google user data is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or transferred to unrelated third parties. Data is handled only as necessary for the owner-requested rclone operations and in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

This website retains no Google user data. Copies created by backup or synchronization remain under the owner's direct control on the owner's computer or Google Drive and can be deleted by the owner at any time.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke this integration's Google account access at any time from Google Account third-party access settings and can remove the corresponding local rclone configuration.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the integration's data practices change. The current revision date appears at the top of this page.
