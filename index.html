Owner-operated utility

Personal Drive Backup

A private, single-user configuration that uses rclone to back up and synchronize files between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive.

Purpose

This integration is used only by its owner for personal file backup, recovery, and synchronization. It is not offered as a service to other users.

How data is handled

  • Operations are initiated by the owner through rclone.
  • Google Drive files and metadata are processed locally on the owner's computer.
  • This website does not collect files, credentials, analytics, or personal information.
  • No Google user data is sold, advertised against, or shared with third parties.
